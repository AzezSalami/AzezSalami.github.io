Our Story

Once upon a time, a brave human had a wild idea — “What if I let AI do almost everything?” And so, this website was born. Built with 98% AI magic (and 2% coffee-fueled human supervision), this site came to life one pixel at a time. The AI worked tirelessly without demanding snacks, bathroom breaks, or vacation days. Sure, the human fixed a typo here and there, but honestly, the bots did most of the heavy lifting. So if you like what you see, thank the algorithms. If you don’t… well, blame the 2%.
